## Q2. Three strongest supporting mathematical results

1. **Quality-Support First-Entry Characterization** and its quality–dimension Pareto staircase: the earliest prefix containing some \(\tau\)-optimal support realizes \(Q(\tau)\), including tied optima and \(Q(0)=B^\dagger\).

The LP barrier \(J^*-L_{LP}>\epsilon_{cert}\) forbids certification even for an exact incumbent using that fixed bound.

Classical LP, flow, and matroid families remain polynomially tractable when exact prefix values are polynomially computable. R2 is appendix-only.

The solver-bound point-identification test is a key **corollary of Q1**, not a fourth independent pillar.

**LP certificate barrier.** In the 240 Phase B scale–alpha–test records, all full reference MIPs and full LP relaxations report `OPTIMAL`. The real-valued full \(J^*\) is not an independent exact-arithmetic oracle, so use the conservative solver-record analogue
\[
\underline\eta_{LP}=L_{full}^{MIP}-L_{full}^{LP}\le J^*-L_{full}^{LP},
\]
conditional on the reported global bounds being numerically valid. For \(\epsilon_{cert}=0.001\), **240/240 have \(\underline\eta_{LP}>0.001\), 0/240 have \(\underline\eta_{LP}\le0.001\)**. The range by scale is: \(N=8\), 51.7479–171.7205; \(N=20\), 19.5932–81.2454; \(N=30\), 18.2590–64.1370; \(N=50\), 12.3130–51.5635 (60 records each). The maximum full incumbent–bound difference is \(4.55\times10^{-13}\), but the conclusion uses the lower bound, not an assumed exact incumbent. Therefore **the fixed full LP bound cannot certify even the true full optimum at this tolerance in any of the 240 records**, subject to bound validity. This directly explains zero *LP-bound* early certificates in this protocol; it does not prove no stronger bound or different relaxation could succeed. The 240 records contain only 80 distinct test-generation seeds reused across three alpha choices, so do not call them 240 independent problem draws. In B.5, all 1,440 saved LP-bearing repetition rows likewise have `full_lb - lp_lb > 0.001` (range 12.3130–171.7205); repetitions are not independent additional instances.

**Exactness/lattice connection.** The locked integer-CFLP audit stores 1,200 `OK` labels (30 replications × 40 calibration/test instances), each with a full solver-reported exact objective and \(B^\dagger\). Integer fixed/open and shipping costs, integer demands/capacities, and total unimodularity of the fixed-opening transportation subproblem imply every *optimal prefix value* lies in \(\mathbb Z\), so distinct mathematical optimal values differ by at least \(h=1\). This does **not** make every arbitrary feasible continuous flow's objective integer. The full saved solver records have `full_gap=0` and integer full incumbent display; prefix solver records are not persisted. The code's `UB-LB<0.95` check and `int(round(UB))` are practical numerical criteria, but **no separate outward-rounding/near-integer verification is implemented or logged for every prefix**. Do not call the saved labels exact-arithmetic proof; T5 must spell out the solver-tolerance assumption or add an independently audited, provenance-preserving numeric verification before asserting lattice-certified exactness. No locked experiment was changed here.

**Compression versus certification.** B.5 saved results show zero early LP certificates and all final certificates sourced from full MIP bounds; warm starts reduce some search measures but the best operational candidate path remains slower than the full-MIP baseline for \(N=20,30,50\). This is evidence that a statistical/structural dimension benefit is not automatically a wall-clock benefit when full certification dominates. The separate small portfolio audit illustrates an application and synthetic coverage, not the newly derived quality-spectrum theorem.

## Q7. Final novelty moat

The defensible, narrow contribution is to make the *minimum nested candidate dimension needed for a specified optimization-quality tolerance* the central object and to characterize exactly what can and cannot be learned about it from incomplete, globally valid solver records. The sharp set can be nonconvex, and the candidate/full bounds yield a necessary-and-sufficient point-ID criterion; support-entry geometry explains the target, while reference and certification translations explain why convenient proxies may not identify it. Established split conformal calibration supplies a distributional budget rule, and the locked experiments expose the gap between candidate compression and certifiable speed. The moat is this **combination and object-specific identification**, not conformal prediction, model reduction, safe screening, envelope arithmetic, or NP-hardness in isolation. Specialist literature review may still narrow it.
